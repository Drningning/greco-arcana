The Role of Devotional Service

← Return to the Library

Every degree of the Silver Temple includes an act of devotion expressed through ordinary life.

These devotions are intentionally quiet.

They are not performed for recognition.
They are not measured by quantity.
They are not reported to a mentor.

Whether one donates a single coin, prepares meals for those in need, plants trees, visits the lonely, or performs another work of mercy, the external act is only part of the discipline.

The greater work is learning to bless the world without seeking credit.

Every symbolic current must eventually become visible through conduct. Otherwise it remains only an interesting idea.
