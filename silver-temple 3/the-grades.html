Stages of Formation

The Grades

The grades of the Silver Temple are stages of formation rather than ranks of authority. Each degree develops a particular capacity within the practitioner while preparing the foundation for the next.

First Grade

Seeker

Establishes the symbolic language and foundational disciplines of the tradition.

Second Grade

Miles

Forms discipline, order, and the sanctification of ordinary life.

Third Grade

Adeptus

Develops inner transformation through deeper ritual and contemplative practice.

Fourth Grade

Epoptes

Cultivates mature spiritual perception, discernment, and direct participation in the mysteries.

Fifth Grade

Hem-Netjer

Completes the mentor-guided path. The initiate returns to ordinary life prepared to embody the tradition rather than withdraw from the world.

The Honorary Dignities

Beyond the initiatory curriculum are three honorary dignities.

Honorary Dignity

Kadosh

Recognizes exceptional stewardship of the Silver Temple.

Honorary Dignity

Katasteros

Honors extraordinary contributions to the Western Mystery Tradition beyond the Temple itself.

Honorary Dignity

Raḥīmā

Recognizes a lifetime devoted to faithfully transmitting the tradition by guiding many others through the complete path.

The grades exist to cultivate maturity, never prestige. Advancement recognizes demonstrated formation rather than time, popularity, or ambition.
