Begin the Path

Request Admission

The Silver Temple accepts a limited number of new initiates.

Admission is based not upon prior knowledge or social standing, but upon sincerity, maturity, and a demonstrated willingness to undertake disciplined personal work.

The First Step Is a Conversation

Prospective applicants are invited to introduce themselves, describe their background, explain what has drawn them to the Western Mystery Tradition, and share why they believe the Silver Temple may be the appropriate path for them.

Every inquiry receives thoughtful consideration.

Because mentorship is personal, admission is limited by the availability of qualified mentors rather than by demand.

The Silver Temple grows slowly by design.

Those who begin the path should do so with the intention of walking it faithfully, one step at a time.

Prospective applicants may also wish to review the Recommended Items that every Seeker gradually gathers for the Work.

If you believe the Silver Temple may be your path, we welcome your inquiry.
