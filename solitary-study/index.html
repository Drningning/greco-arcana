Greco Arcana presents

The Solitary Study

Ad lucem per sapientiam — Toward light through wisdom.

Nine stages. One great tradition.

An independent course of study through Freemasonry, Hermetic philosophy, Qabalah, Rosicrucianism and the Western Mysteries.

Begin Your Studies Explore the Intellectual Map Discover the Council

The mysteries are concealed in plain sight. Learning how to recognize them is an education unto itself.

The Western Mystery Tradition is often encountered in fragments. A book on Tarot. A course in astrology. A volume on ceremonial magic. An introduction to Qabalah. A Masonic degree.

Each contains something of value. Yet without understanding the relationships between them, the student may spend decades collecting pieces of a puzzle without ever seeing the picture they compose.

The Solitary Study was developed to address precisely this problem.

Drawing upon the collective works of philosophers, mystics, Freemasons, Rosicrucians, Qabalists and ceremonial magicians, Greco Arcana presents a recommended course of independent study through the Western Mystery Tradition.

Freemasonry occupies the center of this particular approach, providing the initiatory allegories through which the greater tradition may be investigated.

From there, the student proceeds through the philosophical foundations of Platonism, the cosmology of Hermeticism, the science of correspondences, the architecture of Qabalah, the regenerative mysteries of Rosicrucianism and the operative traditions of ceremonial magic.

Each stage establishes the knowledge necessary to understand what follows.

The objective is not to become proficient in a collection of unrelated occult disciplines.

It is to understand why those disciplines belong together.

An education without an institution

The path is open. The work is yours.

There is no organization to join, no oath to take and no membership to maintain.

The Solitary Study is freely available to anyone willing to undertake the work. Although Freemasonry provides its organizing framework, Masonic membership is not required.

The nine stages represent educational milestones, not degrees conferred by an institution. Completion does not constitute formal initiation, an accredited academic qualification or membership in an esoteric order.

The measure of your progress is not a title bestowed upon you, but your ability to understand, articulate and apply what you have learned.

A recommendation, not a requirement. No membership of any kind is required for The Solitary Study. Because Freemasonry provides its organizing allegory, Greco Arcana recommends that men seek admission to a regular Masonic lodge, and that women look to Universal Co-Masonry or Le Droit Humain. Interested students should inquire directly with the relevant organization about eligibility and admission requirements.

The curriculum

The nine stages

Each stage asks one question and supplies the readings, exercises and standards needed to answer it. Together the stages take about 42 months at a steady pace. The durations are recommendations, not requirements.

  1. I Porta Lucis The Gate of Light Why are the mysteries communicated through initiation, allegory and symbolism? About 3 months ·
  2. II Philosophia The Nature of Reality What is the relationship between visible and invisible reality? About 6 months ·
  3. III Scientia Hermetica The Science of Correspondence How are man, nature and the cosmos connected? About 4 months ·
  4. IV Natura Arcana The Hidden Virtues of Nature How are invisible principles traditionally understood to express themselves through nature? About 4 months ·
  5. V Arbor Vitae The Tree of Life How does unity become multiplicity? About 6 months ·
  6. VI Rosa Crucis The Mystery of Regeneration What must man become through initiation? About 4 months ·
  7. VII Ars Magica The Sacred Art How is esoteric knowledge put into operation? About 8 months ·
  8. VIII Lingua Mysteriorum The Language of the Mysteries How can the greater tradition be expressed and interpreted through images? About 4 months ·
  9. IX Verbum Perditum The Lost Word What was the purpose of initiation? About 3 months ·
