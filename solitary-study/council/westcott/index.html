Inner Council · selected author

William Wynn Westcott

1848–1925

Rosicrucian Freemasonry and Hermetic Qabalah

Inclusion in the Council reflects Greco Arcana's recognition of an author's published contribution to the tradition. It does not imply membership, participation, affiliation, endorsement, sponsorship or approval of Greco Arcana, Joseph Greco or The Solitary Study. About the Council

Biographical overview

A London coroner and Freemason, Westcott was Supreme Magus of the Societas Rosicruciana in Anglia and a co-founder of the Golden Dawn in 1888. He translated the Sepher Yetzirah (1887) and published An Introduction to the Study of the Kabalah (1910).

Contribution to the tradition

Westcott links the Masonic and Rosicrucian societies of the nineteenth century to the founding of the Golden Dawn, and his translations made basic Kabbalistic texts available to English readers.

An Introduction to the Study of the Kabalah

William Wynn Westcott

First published 1910

Why it belongs here
A concise survey written by a founder of the Golden Dawn. It shows what an educated English occultist of 1910 understood the Kabbalah to be.
Investigate while reading
Note where Westcott describes Jewish sources and where he moves into Christian and Hermetic interpretation.

Essential reading · Intermediate · Public domain

Sepher Yetzirah

Anonymous; translated by W. W. Westcott

Sepher Yetzirah: The Book of Formation (selected introductory material)

Hebrew text of uncertain date, usually placed between the 3rd and 6th centuries; Westcott's translation 1887

Where to read
Read the first chapter, on the ten Sefirot and the thirty-two paths of wisdom.
Why it belongs here
The Sefer Yetzirah describes creation through ten numbers and twenty-two letters. It is the oldest source for the structure the later Tree of Life arranges.
Investigate while reading
What are the ten Sefirot in this text, before the later medieval doctrine of divine attributes developed?
Connections
Westcott's and Mathers's works show how the Golden Dawn read this material.
