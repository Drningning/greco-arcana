Christopher Warnock

living author

Traditional astrology and astrological magic

Inclusion in the Council reflects Greco Arcana's recognition of an author's published contribution to the tradition. It does not imply membership, participation, affiliation, endorsement, sponsorship or approval of Greco Arcana, Joseph Greco or The Solitary Study. About the Council

Biographical overview

An American author and teacher of traditional and Renaissance astrology, Warnock is known for his work on astrological talismans and for the English translation of the Picatrix made with John Michael Greer.

Contribution to the tradition

Warnock teaches how traditional astrology was applied to the making of talismans: the selection of image, material and moment. He shows the reasoning behind practices that Ficino and Agrippa describe in theory.

Most significant work in this curriculum

Essential reading · Intermediate

Secrets of Planetary Magic

Christopher Warnock

Renaissance Astrology, 2010

Why it belongs here
Warnock explains how traditional astrology was used to choose the time, image and material of a talisman, putting the theory of Ficino and Agrippa into practice.
Investigate while reading
Follow one planetary talisman from its intention to its election. Which decisions are astrological, which material and which symbolic?
Connections
Applies Ficino and Agrippa. The same planetary logic organizes the paths of the Tree of Life (Stage V) and the Tarot (Stage VIII).
