Inner Council · selected author

Dion Fortune

1890–1946

Hermetic Qabalah and the Western Esoteric Tradition

Inclusion in the Council reflects Greco Arcana's recognition of an author's published contribution to the tradition. It does not imply membership, participation, affiliation, endorsement, sponsorship or approval of Greco Arcana, Joseph Greco or The Solitary Study. About the Council

Biographical overview

Born Violet Mary Firth in Llandudno, Wales, Fortune was initiated into a Golden Dawn successor order and in the 1920s founded the Fraternity of the Inner Light, later the Society of the Inner Light. The Mystical Qabalah appeared in 1935.

Contribution to the tradition

Fortune presented the Hermetic Qabalah as a practical psychology of the soul, written for the English-speaking student. <cite>The Mystical Qabalah</cite> remains one of the clearest introductions to the Tree of Life as it is used in the Western tradition.

Essential reading · Intermediate

The Mystical Qabalah

Dion Fortune

First published 1935

Where to read
Read the opening chapters on general principles before the chapters on the individual Sephiroth.
Why it belongs here
The clearest introduction to the Tree of Life as it is used in the Western tradition, written by a teacher trained in the Golden Dawn current.
Investigate while reading
For each Sephirah, note its divine name, archangel, planet and virtue. Then ask what Fortune adds that is psychological rather than traditional.
