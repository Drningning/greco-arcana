Samuel Liddell MacGregor Mathers

1854–1918

Ceremonial magic and the Golden Dawn

Inclusion in the Council reflects Greco Arcana's recognition of an author's published contribution to the tradition. It does not imply membership, participation, affiliation, endorsement, sponsorship or approval of Greco Arcana, Joseph Greco or The Solitary Study. About the Council

Biographical overview

An English occultist and translator, Mathers co-founded the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn in 1888 with William Wynn Westcott and William Robert Woodman. He published The Kabbalah Unveiled (1887) and editions of the Key of Solomon (1889) and The Book of the Sacred Magic of Abramelin the Mage (1898).

Contribution to the tradition

Mathers wrote much of the Golden Dawn's ritual and teaching material, joining Qabalah, Enochian magic, astrology, Tarot and Rosicrucian symbolism into one ceremonial system. <cite>The Kabbalah Unveiled</cite> translates part of Knorr von Rosenroth's <cite>Kabbala Denudata</cite>.

The Kabbalah Unveiled

Samuel Liddell MacGregor Mathers

First published 1887; translated from Knorr von Rosenroth's Latin Kabbala Denudata

Where to read
Mathers's introduction first; then the Book of Concealed Mystery.
Why it belongs here
Mathers translated Zoharic texts from Knorr von Rosenroth's Christian Latin edition. His introduction became a primary source for the Golden Dawn's Qabalah.
Investigate while reading
Trace the chain of transmission: Aramaic text, Latin Christian edition, English occult translation. What might change at each step?
Connections
