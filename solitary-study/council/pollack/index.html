Rachel Pollack

1945–2023

Tarot interpretation

Inclusion in the Council reflects Greco Arcana's recognition of an author's published contribution to the tradition. It does not imply membership, participation, affiliation, endorsement, sponsorship or approval of Greco Arcana, Joseph Greco or The Solitary Study. About the Council

Biographical overview

An American writer and Tarot scholar, Pollack published Seventy-Eight Degrees of Wisdom in two parts (1980 and 1983) and created the Shining Tribe Tarot.

Contribution to the tradition

Pollack's reading of the Rider–Waite–Smith images is among the most influential modern interpretations of the deck, joining the traditional correspondences with psychological and mythic insight.
