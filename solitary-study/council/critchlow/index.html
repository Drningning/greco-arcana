Outer Council · selected author

Keith Critchlow

1933–2020

Sacred geometry and traditional architecture

Inclusion in the Council reflects Greco Arcana's recognition of an author's published contribution to the tradition. It does not imply membership, participation, affiliation, endorsement, sponsorship or approval of Greco Arcana, Joseph Greco or The Solitary Study. About the Council

Biographical overview

An English artist, architect and teacher, Critchlow wrote Order in Space (1969), Islamic Patterns (1976) and The Hidden Geometry of Flowers (2011), and co-founded the Temenos Academy.

Contribution to the tradition

Critchlow teaches geometry as a contemplative discipline in the Platonic tradition, showing how number and proportion appear in nature and in sacred architecture.

Essential reading · Introductory

The Hidden Geometry of Flowers

Keith Critchlow

The Hidden Geometry of Flowers: Living Rhythms, Form and Number

Floris Books, 2011

Why it belongs here
Critchlow teaches geometry as contemplation: drawing a figure in order to understand the order it expresses. It is the practical companion to the Timaeus.
Investigate while reading
Construct the figures yourself with compass and straightedge before reading the commentary on them.
Connections
Practical companion to the Timaeus. Prepares for the geometrical training in Greer's Way of the Golden Section (Stage VII).
