Gemma Gary

living author

Cornish traditional witchcraft

Inclusion in the Council reflects Greco Arcana's recognition of an author's published contribution to the tradition. It does not imply membership, participation, affiliation, endorsement, sponsorship or approval of Greco Arcana, Joseph Greco or The Solitary Study. About the Council

Biographical overview

A Cornish author and practitioner whose books on the folk magic of Cornwall include Traditional Witchcraft: A Cornish Book of Ways, published by Troy Books.

Contribution to the tradition

Gary records a regional folk tradition with its own logic of place, season and material. It is studied as a living folk practice, not as a descendant of Renaissance occult philosophy.

Most significant work in this curriculum

Supplementary reading · Introductory

Traditional Witchcraft: A Cornish Book of Ways

Gemma Gary

Troy Books, 2008

Why it belongs here
A record of regional folk magic rooted in place, season and the materials of the land. It is read as a living tradition with its own logic, not as a descendant of Agrippa.
