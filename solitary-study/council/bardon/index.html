Inner Council · selected author

Franz Bardon

1909–1958

Hermetic training and evocation

Inclusion in the Council reflects Greco Arcana's recognition of an author's published contribution to the tradition. It does not imply membership, participation, affiliation, endorsement, sponsorship or approval of Greco Arcana, Joseph Greco or The Solitary Study. About the Council

Biographical overview

A Czech teacher of Hermetics from Opava, Bardon published Initiation into Hermetics in German in 1956, followed by The Practice of Magical Evocation and The Key to the True Kabbalah.

Contribution to the tradition

Bardon offers a graded, self-directed training in concentration, elemental balance and magical practice. It is included as an independent operative system rather than as part of the Golden Dawn lineage.
