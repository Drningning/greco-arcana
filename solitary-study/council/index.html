The Council of the Western Mysteries

The selected authors whose published works form the curriculum: philosophers, scholars, mystics and practitioners from Plato to the present.

About the Council

The Council of the Western Mysteries is an independent scholarly and editorial framework conceived by Joseph Greco. Its purpose is to bring together the published works of distinguished philosophers, scholars, mystics and practitioners into a comprehensive course of study through the Western Mystery Tradition.

Inclusion reflects Greco Arcana’s recognition of an author’s intellectual contribution to the tradition. It does not imply membership, participation, affiliation, endorsement, sponsorship or approval of Greco Arcana, Joseph Greco or The Solitary Study curriculum.

The selection and classification of authors, recommended readings and educational interpretations are solely the responsibility of Greco Arcana.

How the Council is organized

The Council is not a list of biographies. Each author is placed by the contribution their work makes to the architecture of the tradition: the questions they opened, the texts they transmitted and the practices they made possible. Inner Council and Outer Council are editorial classifications only. The Inner Council gathers the authors on whom the curriculum principally relies; the Outer Council gathers authors whose works supply foundations, comparisons or practical applications. Neither is a rank, an appointment or a position accepted by any author.
