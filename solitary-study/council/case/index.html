Inner Council · selected author

Paul Foster Case

1884–1954

Hermetic Qabalah, Tarot and Rosicrucian teaching

Inclusion in the Council reflects Greco Arcana's recognition of an author's published contribution to the tradition. It does not imply membership, participation, affiliation, endorsement, sponsorship or approval of Greco Arcana, Joseph Greco or The Solitary Study. About the Council

Biographical overview

An American occultist and musician, Case joined a Golden Dawn successor temple before founding his own teaching order, the Builders of the Adytum. His books include The Tarot: A Key to the Wisdom of the Ages (1947) and The True and Invisible Rosicrucian Order (published 1985).

Contribution to the tradition

Case developed the Qabalistic Tarot into a disciplined method of contemplation, and his Rosicrucian writings connect the Tarot with the grades of the Rosicrucian path.
