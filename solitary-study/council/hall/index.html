Inner Council · selected author

Manly P. Hall

1901–1990

Comparative esoteric philosophy and symbolism

Inclusion in the Council reflects Greco Arcana's recognition of an author's published contribution to the tradition. It does not imply membership, participation, affiliation, endorsement, sponsorship or approval of Greco Arcana, Joseph Greco or The Solitary Study. About the Council

Biographical overview

A Canadian-born American writer and lecturer, Hall published The Lost Keys of Freemasonry in 1923 and the encyclopedic The Secret Teachings of All Ages in 1928. He founded the Philosophical Research Society in Los Angeles in 1934 and lectured for more than six decades.

Contribution to the tradition

Hall introduced generations of readers to the symbolic unity of the Western traditions. His early book on Freemasonry treats the Craft as the custodian of a philosophical inheritance, which is exactly the question Stage I asks the student to examine.

Essential reading · Introductory · Public domain

The Lost Keys of Freemasonry

Manly P. Hall

The Lost Keys of Freemasonry, or The Secret of Hiram Abiff

First published 1923

Why it belongs here
A short, devotional reading of the Craft as a philosophical inheritance. Hall asks what the builder must do to become the temple, which is the question that sends the student into the rest of the curriculum.
Investigate while reading
Compare Hall's account of Hiram Abiff with the plain legend of the third degree. What has Hall added, and why?
Connections
Read alongside Wilmshurst. Hall's claim that Masonry preserves an older philosophy is tested in Stages II and III.
