Albert G. Mackey

1807–1881

Masonic symbolism and jurisprudence

Inclusion in the Council reflects Greco Arcana's recognition of an author's published contribution to the tradition. It does not imply membership, participation, affiliation, endorsement, sponsorship or approval of Greco Arcana, Joseph Greco or The Solitary Study. About the Council

Biographical overview

A physician from Charleston, South Carolina, Mackey was one of the most influential Masonic scholars of the nineteenth century. He published The Symbolism of Freemasonry in 1869 and An Encyclopaedia of Freemasonry in 1873.

Contribution to the tradition

Mackey explains why Freemasonry teaches through symbol, allegory and the legend of the lost word. His treatment of symbolism is the foundation on which Stage I is built.

The Symbolism of Freemasonry

Albert G. Mackey

The Symbolism of Freemasonry: Illustrating and Explaining Its Science and Philosophy, Its Legends, Myths and Symbols

First published 1869; revised editions followed

Where to read
Read the opening chapters on the nature of symbolism, then the chapters on the legend of the third degree and the lost word.
Why it belongs here
Mackey explains why Freemasonry teaches through symbol and allegory rather than plain statement. Every later stage assumes the student can read a symbol in this way.
Investigate while reading
How does Mackey distinguish a symbol from an allegory, and why does he believe the ancient mysteries taught by both?
Connections
Wilmshurst takes Mackey's symbolic method inward, reading the same symbols as stages of the soul's development.
