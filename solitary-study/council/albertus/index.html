Outer Council · selected author

Albertus Magnus

c. 1200–1280

Medieval natural philosophy

Inclusion in the Council reflects Greco Arcana's recognition of an author's published contribution to the tradition. It does not imply membership, participation, affiliation, endorsement, sponsorship or approval of Greco Arcana, Joseph Greco or The Solitary Study. About the Council

Biographical overview

A German Dominican friar, bishop and teacher of Thomas Aquinas, Albertus wrote on almost every branch of natural philosophy, including the Book of Minerals (De mineralibus). He was canonized in 1931.

Contribution to the tradition

Albertus treated the hidden powers of stones, plants and animals as a subject of natural philosophy. Agrippa cites him, and several later books of secrets circulated under his name.
