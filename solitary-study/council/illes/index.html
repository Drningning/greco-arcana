Outer Council · selected author

Judika Illes

living author

Folk magic and spellcraft

Inclusion in the Council reflects Greco Arcana's recognition of an author's published contribution to the tradition. It does not imply membership, participation, affiliation, endorsement, sponsorship or approval of Greco Arcana, Joseph Greco or The Solitary Study. About the Council

Biographical overview

An American author whose reference works on magical traditions include The Encyclopedia of 5,000 Spells (2004).

Contribution to the tradition

Illes documents folk and practical magic from many cultures. Her work shows how widely correspondence-based practice spread, and why folk traditions should not be assumed to descend from learned magic.

Most significant work in this curriculum

Supplementary reading · Introductory

The Encyclopedia of 5,000 Spells

Judika Illes

The Element Encyclopedia of 5000 Spells

HarperElement, 2004

Why it belongs here
A broad survey of folk and practical magic from many cultures. It shows that correspondence-based practice is widespread and that much of it developed independently of Renaissance occult philosophy.
Investigate while reading
Look for practices from different cultures that share a logic of correspondence. Avoid assuming that similarity proves a common origin.
