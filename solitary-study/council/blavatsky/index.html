Helena P. Blavatsky

1831–1891

Theosophy and comparative esoteric cosmology

Inclusion in the Council reflects Greco Arcana's recognition of an author's published contribution to the tradition. It does not imply membership, participation, affiliation, endorsement, sponsorship or approval of Greco Arcana, Joseph Greco or The Solitary Study. About the Council

Biographical overview

Born in Ekaterinoslav in the Russian Empire, Blavatsky co-founded the Theosophical Society in New York in 1875 with Henry Steel Olcott and William Quan Judge. Her major works are Isis Unveiled (1877) and The Secret Doctrine (1888).

Contribution to the tradition

Blavatsky popularized a comparative esoteric cosmology that drew Eastern and Western sources into one system and shaped much of the twentieth-century occult revival. In this curriculum she is read comparatively, as an influential esoteric cosmology rather than an authority on traditional Jewish Kabbalah.
