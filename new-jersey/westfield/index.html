Westfield holds an important place in my initiatic life

Westfield is the home of my Royal Arch Chapter and the temple where I received several important degrees, including Mark Master Mason, Most Excellent Master, Past Master (Virtual), and the Holy Royal Arch. For me, Westfield is not simply a beautiful town in Union County. It is a place tied to ritual, ascent, symbolism, and the search for the hidden word — and that history naturally informs the work I offer through Greco Arcana.

For clients in Westfield I offer hermetic tarot readings, ceremonial magic, spiritual alchemical consultations, talismans, amulets, ritual object consecration, and banishing rituals. The work is rooted in the Western Mystery Tradition and designed for people who want more than a surface-level reading.

Tarot for crossroads, patterns, and decisions

Many clients come to tarot during a major transition: a relationship decision, a business change, a spiritual pressure, a creative block, a new home, or a repeating pattern they cannot fully explain. A hermetic tarot reading helps reveal the symbolic structure beneath the situation. It does not replace your will, judgment, or responsibility; it clarifies the forces at play so you can act with greater awareness.

Serving Westfield and Union County

Greco Arcana serves Westfield and the surrounding Union County area, including Cranford, Scotch Plains, Fanwood, Garwood, Clark, Colonia, and nearby towns. This is my immediate local region — the area around Scotch Plains is long associated with the Atlas Pythagoras lodge tradition, and the towns near Colonia are connected to the work of the Silver Temple, a serious esoteric and initiatic current rooted in symbolic study, ritual practice, and the Western Mystery Tradition. Because these towns are so close to home, in-person readings, private events, or ritual consultations are often possible by arrangement, and online sessions are always available.

People usually come to this work when they are standing at a threshold: a career decision, a relationship crossroads, a business transition, a spiritual blockage, a new home, a sacred object in need of consecration, or a pattern of signs and pressures they can feel but cannot yet name.
