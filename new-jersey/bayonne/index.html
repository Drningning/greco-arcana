Bayonne is sacred ground to me

Bayonne is the town where I grew up, and it is also the home of my mother lodge in Freemasonry, Peninsula No. 99. It was there that I went through my initiation, received my degrees, learned ritual in the evenings, and spent long nights in the temple with brothers, books, symbols, and the kind of silence that teaches more than words.

So when I offer tarot readings, ceremonial magic, spiritual alchemy, and ritual services in Bayonne, this is not just another local service page. Bayonne is part of my own initiatic story.

Through Greco Arcana I offer serious metaphysical work rooted in hermetic tarot, Qabalah, astrology, Freemasonry, ritual magic, and the Western Mystery Tradition. These readings are not fortune-telling for entertainment. They are meant for people facing a real crossroads, a spiritual pressure, a symbolic pattern, or a decision that needs more than ordinary advice.

Services available in Bayonne

Greco Arcana offers hermetic tarot readings, spiritual alchemical consultations, ritual object consecrations, custom talismans and amulets, banishing rituals, and metaphysical guidance for seekers in Bayonne and the surrounding Hudson County area. Sessions can be done online, and in-person readings or ritual events may be available depending on location, timing, and the nature of the work.

Why people come

People usually come to this work when they are standing at a threshold: a career decision, a relationship crossroads, a business transition, a spiritual blockage, a new home, a sacred object in need of consecration, or a pattern of signs and pressures they can feel but cannot yet name. The work is designed to help you see the pattern beneath the situation.
