A city I have long called home ground

Hoboken is one of the places I have lived and worked over the years, and I know its streets and its rhythm well. That familiarity matters: this is not work I offer to an abstract market, but to a city that has been part of my own life in New Jersey.

Through Greco Arcana I offer hermetic tarot readings, ceremonial magic, spiritual alchemy, talismans and amulets, and ritual object consecration for seekers in Hoboken and throughout Hudson County. The work is rooted in the Western Mystery Tradition — Hermeticism, Qabalah, astrology, Freemasonry, numerology, and ceremonial magic — and it is meant for people who want more than a surface-level reading.

Tarot for crossroads and decisions

Hoboken is a city of ambitious, busy lives, and the questions I am asked here often reflect that: career moves, business transitions, relationships, timing, and the deeper pattern beneath a decision. A hermetic tarot reading does not replace your judgment; it clarifies the forces at play so you can act with greater awareness.

People usually come to this work when they are standing at a threshold: a career decision, a relationship crossroads, a business transition, a spiritual blockage, a new home, a sacred object in need of consecration, or a pattern of signs and pressures they can feel but cannot yet name.

Services available in Hoboken

Greco Arcana offers hermetic tarot readings, the flagship Hermetic Praxis Reading, banishing rituals for homes and offices, custom talismans and amulets, and deck and shewstone consecration. Sessions are available online, with in-person work by arrangement.
