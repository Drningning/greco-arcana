Jersey City has deep personal meaning for me

I have a long history of Freemasonry in Jersey City and many good friends inside the halls of its lodges. It is a place where ritual, fraternity, symbolism, and spiritual study have all had a real presence in my life, and that background shapes the way I approach tarot, ceremonial magic, and metaphysical work.

This work is not casual entertainment. It is for people who feel that something deeper is moving beneath the surface of their life. Sometimes that appears as a decision, sometimes as a pattern, sometimes as pressure, repetition, intuition, or a sense that the outer situation has an inner meaning. Hermetic tarot is especially useful when life feels symbolic but unclear: the cards become a map of the unseen structure beneath the visible problem.

A serious approach to tarot and ritual

My approach draws from the Western Mystery Tradition — Hermeticism, Qabalah, astrology, Freemasonry, numerology, and ceremonial magic. A reading is not just a list of predictions; it is an interpretation of symbols, timing, archetypes, and spiritual forces. For clients in Jersey City, readings can be done online, and in-person sessions or events may be available by arrangement.

Services available in Jersey City

Greco Arcana offers hermetic tarot readings, spiritual alchemical consultations, custom talismans and amulets, ritual object consecration, banishing rituals, and spiritual guidance for major transitions.
