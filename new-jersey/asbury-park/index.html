A Museum Dedicated to the Unexplained

Among New Jersey's many seaside communities, Asbury Park has cultivated a particularly unusual relationship with the supernatural.

In the heart of downtown, Paranormal Books & Curiosities and its companion Paranormal Museum have welcomed visitors since 2008.

Founded by historian Kathy Kelly, the museum explores supernatural folklore, unusual artifacts, ghost stories, and the mysteries that have fascinated generations of curious people.

Here, the paranormal isn't an occasional Halloween attraction.

It's part of the city's year-round cultural life.

The organization also conducts historical and ghost tours, introducing visitors to stories of the boardwalk, the city's older buildings, maritime disasters, and reported supernatural encounters.

Asbury Park has embraced its strange history with a refreshing combination of curiosity and good storytelling.

The Ghosts Along the Boardwalk

The city's waterfront has witnessed extraordinary changes.

Grand hotels rose and declined. The boardwalk evolved. Storms battered the coast. Fires and maritime disasters became part of local memory.

One particularly dramatic chapter came in 1934 when the burning ocean liner SS Morro Castle grounded off the Asbury Park shoreline.

The disaster claimed 137 lives.

The sight of the enormous, blackened ship looming offshore became an unforgettable part of New Jersey history.

The Morro Castle tragedy also entered the city's ghost-tour narratives, where accounts of real disasters stand beside reported unexplained occurrences.

Not every mystery requires a ghost to be haunting. Sometimes the historical event itself is unsettling enough.
