The Ghost Who Couldn't Stop Sneezing

Not every ghost announces its presence with rattling chains, slamming doors, or mournful cries.

One of Montclair's most curious ghosts apparently preferred to sneeze.

In December 1961, The Montclair Times published a remarkable account concerning a house at 149 Watchung Avenue. Its inhabitants described hearing a sneeze they attributed to a former resident named Christian Interest.

There was just one difficulty.

Christian Interest had been dead since September 29, 1823.

Interest was a German-born immigrant whose life had included service in the British army before the American Revolution. According to local history, he eventually supported the Revolutionary cause and feared being captured by Hessian soldiers.

His solution was to create a secret hiding place in the cellar of his house.

Interest reportedly lived to the age of eighty and even dug his own grave.

Long afterward, the homeowners claimed that his unmistakable sneeze could still be heard in the house.

One resident, Mary Travis Arny, acknowledged that there might be an entirely ordinary explanation for the sound, but preferred to leave the mystery undisturbed.

And really, who could blame her?

Most haunted houses are rather less cheerful about their inhabitants.

The Spirits of Tierney's Tavern

Montclair has other supernatural traditions.

Tierney's Tavern, a longtime neighborhood gathering place on Valley Road, has acquired stories of unexplained footsteps, whispers, shadowy figures, and strange sounds after closing.

One particular legend concerns a chair associated with a regular patron who died in 1982.

Like many historic taverns, Tierney's has become a gathering place for both living customers and ghost stories.

Neither tale establishes the existence of a spirit, but both are genuine elements of Montclair's local folklore.
