A New Jersey practice rooted in the Western mysteries.

The work of Greco Arcana is conducted from New Jersey, where Joseph Greco was initiated through the Blue Lodge, the Scottish Rite, the Holy Royal Arch, and the Cryptic Council. His clients across the state, from Morristown to Bay Head, from Jersey City to Cranford, include brothers of the Craft, professionals at crossroads of consequence, and serious students of the perennial philosophy.

New Jersey has long held a quiet but significant role in the American esoteric tradition. The Scottish Rite has been at home here for over a century, and the state remains a meeting ground for serious practitioners of Freemasonry, Rosicrucianism, and the Hermetic arts. The work offered through Greco Arcana is offered in that same tradition, grounded, structured, and free of New Age generality.
