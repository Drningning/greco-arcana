A natural resonance with study and tradition

Princeton has a natural resonance with study, tradition, philosophy, and serious inquiry. It is also a place where I have worked with the Golden Section Fellowship, whose emphasis on sacred geometry, earth mysteries, and symbolic structure has influenced my own path.

For clients in Princeton, Greco Arcana offers hermetic tarot readings, spiritual alchemical consultations, ceremonial magic, talismans, amulets, ritual object consecration, and banishing rituals. This work is for seekers who want more than a shallow reading — people interested in the symbolic, initiatic, and spiritual dimensions of life.

Tarot as a symbolic system

Hermetic tarot is a complete symbolic language. The cards are connected to Qabalah, astrology, numerology, elemental forces, and archetypal pattern, and when read properly they reveal the deeper architecture beneath a decision, a conflict, a transition, or a spiritual pressure. A reading does not remove your free will; it sharpens your perception.

People usually come to this work when they are standing at a threshold: a career decision, a relationship crossroads, a business transition, a spiritual blockage, a new home, a sacred object in need of consecration, or a pattern of signs and pressures they can feel but cannot yet name.
