Objects
of Power

The extraordinary things that history has lost.

Throughout the centuries, kings, magicians, warriors and seekers have pursued objects believed to possess powers beyond ordinary understanding.

Some were said to command spirits. Others bestowed sovereignty, revealed hidden knowledge or granted access to the divine.

Their stories survive in ancient manuscripts, sacred texts, folklore and historical accounts. Their whereabouts, however, remain among the great mysteries of the world.

Here, we assemble the surviving evidence, examine the clues and explore the conditions under which these extraordinary objects were said to be attained.

Objects of Power

The Catalogue of Lost Treasures

Across civilizations and throughout the centuries, extraordinary objects have appeared in the accounts of kings, priests, warriors, magicians and mystics.

Some vanished with the fall of kingdoms. Others were concealed, stolen or returned to the mysterious forces from which they came. Some were said to reveal themselves only to those worthy of possessing them.

Their stories remain. Their whereabouts are another matter.

The following catalogue represents an ongoing investigation into the world's most extraordinary missing and legendary treasures.
