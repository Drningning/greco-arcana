GRECO ARCANA - SITE UPDATE - 2026-06-26
========================================

HOW TO DEPLOY
-------------
Unzip greco-arcana-update-2026-06-26.zip. Inside is the full site folder
(the same structure as your repo root). In GitHub: Add file > Upload files,
then drag in the CONTENTS of the unzipped folder (index.html, netlify.toml,
sitemap.xml, the services/ folder, the new-jersey/ folder, etc.), and commit.
Uploading overwrites the matching files and adds the two new town folders.
Netlify rebuilds in about a minute.

You can safely upload everything. The footer link changed on every page, so
every page is included in this update.
