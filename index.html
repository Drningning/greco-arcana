The Practitioner

An initiate, not
an entertainer.

Joseph Greco is a practitioner of the Western esoteric tradition, working at the intersection of tarot, astrology, numerology, and ritual arts. His work is rooted in a long-standing initiatic path through systems including Hermeticism, Rosicrucianism, Qabalah, and the symbolic sciences of alchemy and sacred geometry.

What began as the study of separate disciplines revealed itself, over time, as a single current—an integrated tradition often referred to as the Perennial Philosophy. Rather than approaching these arts in isolation, Joseph works within their underlying unity, using multiple systems together to reveal consistent patterns, themes, and direction.

His training includes apprenticeships and formal study in tarot, ceremonial magic, astrological practice, and the broader Western mystery tradition, supported by years of disciplined work, mentorship, and direct experience. The result is a structured, multi-system approach designed to provide clear, grounded insight—helping clients better understand their situation, their patterns, and the forces shaping both.

This practice is not oriented toward general entertainment. It is designed for individuals seeking clear, structured insight at meaningful points of decision, transition, or realignment. Engagements are selective and focused. The work is direct, integrative, and grounded in years of disciplined study, apprenticeship, and application.

Offerings

Diagnosis. Prescription.
Execution.

The work moves in three turnings. The reading reveals the structure of what is. The talisman or amulet is wrought to your specific case. The ritual brings the work into action in the world.

Hermetic Tarot Reading

— the diagnosis

Multiple spreads, customized to the question you actually need answered. Drawn from the Western Mystery tradition rather than New Age generality. Whether you arrive with one situation or a full life-survey, you leave with structure.

The Hermetic Praxis Reading

— the synthesis

The flagship offering: tarot integrated with natal astrology, personal numerology, gematria, and herbal alchemy in a 90-minute working. For those serious enough to want the entire diagnostic apparatus brought to bear at once.

Talismans & Amulets

— the prescription

Bespoke alchemical objects, wrought to address what the reading uncovered. Each is consecrated by ceremony at planetary hour, with the herbal and metallic correspondences proper to your specific working. Not jewelry. Instruments.

Ceremonial Ritual Work

— the execution

Banishing rituals to clear what does not serve. Consecrations of new tarot decks, shewstones, and ritual implements. House work for those who feel the territory is unsettled. The work is performed properly, not theatrically.

The cards do not predict; they describe. Astrology does not dictate; it reveals timing. The talisman does not perform magic upon the world—it teaches the soul to remember its own authority.
