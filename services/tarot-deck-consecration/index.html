Tarot Deck Consecration

— A new deck made your own.

A full ceremonial consecration ritual to make a new deck your own. Performed at appropriate planetary hour with attendant invocations, dedicating the instrument to its work.

Why Consecrate a Deck

A new tarot deck is, until it is consecrated, an inert collection of printed cards. The consecration ceremony transforms the deck into an instrument: dedicated to its work, aligned to its tradition, and rooted in the operator who will employ it.

The ceremony draws from the working methods of the Western Mystery Tradition. It is appropriate for any serious deck — whether you have just acquired your first Rider-Waite-Smith, a Thoth deck, a Marseille, or a Golden Dawn deck — and the consecration is tailored to the symbolic system of the deck in question.

What the Consecration Includes

  • Consultation to understand the deck and its intended use
  • Calculation of the proper planetary hour and astrological conditions
  • Full ceremonial consecration with appropriate invocations
  • Written guide to the care, storage, and use of the consecrated deck

How It Works

The consecration may be performed on-site (anywhere in New Jersey or the greater New York region) or by remote ceremony, with the deck present in your home during the working.
