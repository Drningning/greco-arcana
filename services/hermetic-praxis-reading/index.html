Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Hermetic Praxis Reading, and how is it different from a standard reading?
It's my flagship: a 90-minute consultation combining hermetic tarot, natal astrology, numerology, gematria, and herbal alchemy to examine a major question from several symbolic angles. A standard hermetic reading focuses on the cards — the pattern around a question, the forces and obstacles at work, the likely direction. The Praxis Reading goes further, connecting the situation to your deeper spiritual architecture: your birth chart, numerical pattern, planetary emphasis, and the alchemical qualities that can bring it into balance. The aim isn't only interpretation but *praxis* — moving from diagnosis to prescription.
How do the five components come together in one reading?
Each looks at the question through a different doorway. Tarot reveals the living pattern — what's active, hidden, blocked, or asking to be integrated. Natal astrology shows your constitutional pattern: the planetary and elemental forces shaping temperament, timing, gifts, and recurring themes. Numerology identifies the structure beneath the question. Gematria exposes hidden links between names, words, and spiritual themes through numerical correspondence. Herbal alchemy brings it into practice, identifying the correspondences and material supports that can resolve or strengthen the situation. Tarot shows the pattern, astrology shows the person, numerology shows the structure, gematria reveals the hidden links, and herbal alchemy translates insight into action.
What makes it worth $400?
This is my most complete and intensive consultation, and the depth buys preparation, synthesis, and prescription. Before we meet I review your birth information, weigh the symbolic nature of your question, select the appropriate spreads, and prepare the session as one unified consultation. We aren't simply pulling cards and trading impressions — we build a complete symbolic diagnosis and then identify practical support. The value is in the synthesis: rather than several disconnected interpretations, you receive one integrated reading that brings multiple hermetic sciences into conversation.
What do you need from me beforehand, and why?
Your full birth date, exact birth time, and birth location. The time matters because it sets your rising sign, house placements, and chart angles; without an accurate time, parts of the chart become unreliable. I also ask for the main concern you want to explore, so I can choose the right spreads and prepare with the correct symbolic emphasis. The more clearly the question is framed, the stronger the reading.
What does the herbal alchemy element involve?
It doesn't mean every client automatically leaves with a physical product — it means the reading includes an alchemical prescription layer. Based on what emerges, I may identify herbs, planetary correspondences, virtues, colors, prayers, ritual actions, or symbolic materials that support your situation: solar support for confidence and visibility, lunar or Venusian for grief and emotional healing, Saturnian for discipline, boundaries, and protection. From there I may recommend a talisman, herbal bath, incense formula, prayer practice, or cleansing work. The purpose isn't superstition; it's alignment — giving the insight somewhere to land in the physical world.
Do I leave with anything tangible?
Yes — a clear set of recommendations drawn from the reading. Depending on the session, that may include notes on the tarot pattern, natal chart themes, relevant numbers and correspondences, planetary influences, and herbal or ritual recommendations, along with possible next steps such as a talisman, cleansing, prayer practice, or timing window. The deliverables depend on your question, but the goal is constant: you leave with clarity, direction, and something practical to work with.
Who is this reading really for?
Someone standing at a serious threshold — a major decision, a spiritual crossroads, a business launch, a recurring relationship pattern, an initiatory shift, a period of transformation. It's for the person who senses their question is not merely practical but symbolic, and who wants a complete hermetic analysis rather than a single-question reading. Choose a standard reading for clarity on a specific question; choose the Praxis Reading when the situation matters deeply, when the pattern keeps repeating, or when you suspect the question is tied to a much larger spiritual architecture.
