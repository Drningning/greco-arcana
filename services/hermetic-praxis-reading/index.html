The Hermetic Praxis Reading

— The full diagnostic apparatus brought to bear on a single life.

The integrated working — tarot, natal astrology, personal numerology, gematria, and herbal alchemy combined into one continuous 90-minute reading. For those who have moved beyond curiosity and have arrived at a real question.

What Is Integrated in the Praxis Reading

Hermetic Tarot

Multiple spreads, drawn from the Western Mystery tradition, customized to the questions you bring.

Natal Astrology

Your full natal chart is calculated and read — not as personality description, but as the timing and structure within which the tarot's reading takes place. Time of birth and place of birth are required in advance.

Personal Numerology

Your name and birth date analyzed through Pythagorean numerology and the Qabalistic system, revealing the numerical signatures shaping your current cycle.

Gematria

Where appropriate, Hebrew gematria is applied to names, words, and symbols arising in the reading — the same technique used by the qabalistic tradition to reveal hidden correspondence.

Herbal Alchemy

Plant and herbal correspondences relevant to the planetary forces and conditions uncovered in the reading. Where a talisman or amulet is appropriate, the herbal prescription is included.

What You Receive

  • A 90-minute live reading, in person or by Zoom
  • Written notes on the natal chart and numerology, prepared in advance
  • Any indicated correspondences (planetary, herbal, gemological)
  • A clear synthesis — not raw data, but a structured reading of your situation

Preparation

Upon booking, you will be asked to provide your full name, date, time, and place of birth. The natal chart and numerology are prepared in advance so that the full 90 minutes of the session can be devoted to the reading itself.
