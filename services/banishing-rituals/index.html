Banishing Rituals for Homes & Businesses

— Clear the territory before establishing your own.

A banishing ritual clears a space of accumulated influence — psychic, emotional, and energetic residue that builds up in any place humans have lived, worked, or struggled. Performed on-site throughout New Jersey and the greater New York region.

When to Consider a Banishing

  • Before moving into a new home or rental. Clear the imprint of previous occupants before establishing your own.
  • After a separation, illness, or death in the home. Reset the space when the major event has passed.
  • Before closing on a real estate purchase or sale. Joseph has been engaged for this work by realtors, buyers, and investors throughout New Jersey.
  • For office spaces, particularly after a difficult tenancy. Commercial spaces accumulate influence as readily as residential ones.
  • When the territory simply feels off. If you have read this far, you already know what that means.

What the Working Includes

  • Initial consultation to understand the space and its history
  • Full Lesser Banishing Ritual of the Pentagram performed on-site
  • Ancillary work appropriate to the territory — additional pentagrams, hexagrams, water and incense, salt, and other implements as the case requires
  • Consecration of the space at the conclusion of the banishing
  • Written notes on follow-up care of the space, where appropriate

Service Area

Banishing rituals are performed on-site, with the territory present. Joseph serves clients throughout New Jersey and the greater New York region. For homes outside this area, please inquire about travel arrangements.
